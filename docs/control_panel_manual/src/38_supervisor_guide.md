# 38. Shift Supervisor's Guide

The shift supervisor runs the shop floor during the shift. This chapter collects the supervisor's routine and the decisions the supervisor must take.

## 38.1 Daily routine

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 15 minutes before shift | Read previous shift log, open breakdowns, open LOTO, quality holds; check attendance |
| Shift start | Morning meeting (10 minutes): safety point, yesterday's output and problems, today's plan (Chapter 4.8) |
| First hour | Walk all machines: check sheets filled and signed, first-offs approved, PPE in use |
| Every 2 hours | Production board updated (plan versus actual); help where behind |
| During shift | Respond to andon calls (yellow: material or quality; red: maintenance); approve first-offs; handle holds |
| Last hour | Check cleaning and 5S; collect production records; prepare handover |
| Shift end | Handover to incoming supervisor in person; shift log completed and signed |

## 38.2 Production board

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| Hour | 08-09, 09-10, ... |
| Plan | Planned output (parts, panels, operations) |
| Actual | Actual output |
| Difference | Plan minus actual |
| Reason for loss | Breakdown (asset code), material shortage, quality problem, absence, set-up, other |
| Action | What was done, by whom |

Losses written on the board are reviewed in the next morning meeting. Repeated reasons are taken to the weekly production review.

## 38.3 Handling a breakdown

1. Make the machine safe (stop, LOTO if anyone must enter the danger zone).
2. Call maintenance with the asset code, symptom and alarm code.
3. Inform planning if the stoppage will affect a delivery (critical machines: after 30 minutes).
4. Move operators to other work (cleaning, deburring, training, other machines) so they are not idle.
5. Consider rerouting: some laser parts can be made on PUNCH-02 and vice versa; some bends can be made in a different sequence. Check with the inspector that rerouted parts meet the drawing.
6. After repair: trial run, first-off approval, record in the shift log.

### 38.3.1 Escalation

| Downtime of a critical machine | Inform |
|---|---|
| 30 minutes | Maintenance supervisor, planning |
| 2 hours | Maintenance manager, production manager |
| 4 hours | Plant head; decide on outsourcing or overtime |

## 38.4 Handling a quality problem

1. Stop the process if defects are being produced.
2. Put HOLD tags on suspect parts; segregate.
3. Inform the quality inspector; raise an NCR (Chapter 21.3).
4. Check how far back the problem goes (since the last good check) and hold those parts too.
5. Restart only after the cause is corrected and a new first-off is approved.

## 38.5 Handling an injury or near miss

1. Make the area safe; give first aid; call the first aider and, if needed, the ambulance.
2. Inform the safety officer and the production manager immediately for any injury.
3. Do not disturb the scene (except to make safe) until the safety officer has seen it, for serious incidents.
4. Record the event: who, what, where, when, witnesses, immediate cause.
5. Take part in the investigation and make sure the corrective actions are done.
6. For near misses, collect the near miss card and discuss it at the next morning meeting.

## 38.6 Manpower and skills

- Allocate operators only to machines for which they are authorised (skill level 2 or above; level 1 only with a trainer).
- Use the skill matrix to plan for absence: each critical machine must have a level 3 operator in each shift.
- Plan training slots in quiet periods.
- Rotate operators on repetitive or noisy jobs.

## 38.7 Supervisor's checklist for safe shifts

| Check | Frequency |
|---|---|
| All guards in place, no bypassed safety devices | Each machine walk |
| PPE in use (especially gloves at sheet handling, ear protection at punching, goggles at tanks) | Each walk |
| Walkways and fire equipment clear | Each walk |
| Crane pre-use check done | Shift start |
| LOTO locks: every lock has an owner present or a recorded handover | Shift start and end |
| Hot work permits open: closed with fire watch completed | Shift end |
| Chemicals stored correctly | Daily |
| No one working alone on live equipment | Always |

!! HINGLISH: Supervisor shift ka zimmedaar hai: meeting, machine walk, first-off approval, production board, breakdown aur quality problem sambhalna, handover. Critical machine 30 minute band rahe to maintenance supervisor aur planning ko, 2 ghante par managers ko, 4 ghante par plant head ko batao. Chot lage to pehle first aid, phir safety officer ko turant batao. Sirf authorised operator ko hi machine do.
