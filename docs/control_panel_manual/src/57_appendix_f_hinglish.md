# Appendix F. Shop Floor Guide in Hinglish

Ye appendix shop floor par kaam karne wale operators, helpers aur naye technicians ke liye hai. Isme har machine aur har kaam ka chhota saar (summary) simple Hinglish mein diya gaya hai. Poori aur pakki jaankari ke liye hamesha English chapter padho. English instruction aur Hinglish note mein farak lage to English instruction follow karo aur supervisor ko batao.

## F.1 Suraksha ke 10 niyam

1. Area ke hisaab se PPE hamesha pehno: safety shoes, safety glasses, gloves.
2. Guard, interlock, light curtain ya emergency stop ko kabhi hatao, bypass ya mute mat karo.
3. Machine ya panel ke andar kaam se pehle LOTO: band karo, apna lock aur tag lagao, energy nikalo, check karo.
4. Wahi machine chalao jiski training aur authorisation hai.
5. Har chot, near miss aur khatarnaak halat usi shift mein supervisor ko batao.
6. Machine chalate waqt aur crane zone mein mobile phone nahi.
7. Building ke andar smoking nahi; paint shop mein koi aag ya chingari nahi.
8. Raasta, fire extinguisher, emergency exit aur electrical panel ke saamne jagah khaali rakho.
9. Nashe mein ya neend wali dawai lekar kaam par mat aao.
10. Shak ho to kaam roko. Har karmchari ko unsafe kaam rokne ka haq aur zimmedari hai.

## F.2 LOTO aasaan bhasha mein

| Kadam | Kya karna hai |
|---|---|
| 1 Taiyari | Machine mein kaun si energy hai (bijli, hydraulic, air, gravity, garmi, gas) aur kahan se band hoti hai, ye pata karo |
| 2 Batao | Operator aur supervisor ko batao ki machine band hogi |
| 3 Band karo | Normal stop se machine roko |
| 4 Alag karo | Main switch off, air valve band, pump band, gas valve band |
| 5 Lock aur tag | Har jagah apna laal lock aur tag. Kai log hain to hasp par sabka apna lock |
| 6 Energy nikalo | Ram ko block par utaro, pressure aur air nikalo, capacitor discharge hone do, garam part thande hone do |
| 7 Check karo | Start button daba ke dekho kuch nahi chalta; tester se test-test-test |
| 8 Kaam aur hatao | Kaam ke baad guard lagao, sabko bahar karo, apna lock khud hatao |

## F.6 Kuch zaroori "kabhi nahi"

- Kabhi kisi aur ka lock mat kholo.
- Kabhi light curtain ya emergency stop bypass mat karo.
- Kabhi CT ka secondary khula mat chhodo.
- Kabhi bina ferrule stranded wire screw terminal mein mat lagao.
- Kabhi hydraulic leak haath se mat dhundho.
- Kabhi compressed air kisi insaan ki taraf mat chalao.
- Kabhi purani revision ki drawing se kaam mat karo.
- Kabhi expire calibration wala instrument use mat karo.
- Kabhi panel mein tool ya bolt chhod ke band mat karo.
- Kabhi load ke neeche mat khade ho.

!! HINGLISH: Is manual ki har baat ka maksad hai: aap surakshit rahein, panel sahi bane aur machine lambi chale. Kuch samajh na aaye to poochne mein sharm mat karo.
