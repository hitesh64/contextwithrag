## E.13 Panels in service and live work

**Q161. An ACB will not close. What are the first checks?**
Position indicator at "connected" (or "test"), spring "charged", voltage on the under-voltage release, trip indicator reset, mechanical interlock with the other breaker, closing circuit voltage (Chapter 47.1).

**Q162. May an ACB that tripped on short circuit be closed again at once?**
No. Find the cause and inspect the breaker first; read the trip history from the release (Chapter 47.1).

**Q163. An MCCB trips instantly every time a transformer feeder is switched on. Likely cause?**
The instantaneous setting is too low for the transformer inrush current, or there is a real short circuit; measure starting current and IR before changing any setting (Chapter 47.2).

**Q164. A meter shows negative kW on one phase. Why?**
The CT polarity on that phase is reversed, or the CT and voltage input of that meter element belong to different phases (Chapter 47.5).

**Q165. Lamps glow faintly when switched off on long control cables. Why?**
Induced voltage from capacitive coupling on long cables (Chapter 47.6).

**Q166. What does a discoloured sleeve near a busbar joint indicate?**
An overheating joint; plan a shutdown, clean and re-torque it (Chapter 47.9).

**Q167. Which repairs are never done live?**
Repairs, re-termination, tightening of busbar joints and replacement of devices (Chapter 42.3).

**Q168. What class of insulating gloves is used for 415 V power circuits?**
Class 0 (1000 V) with leather protectors; class 00 (500 V) for 110 / 230 V control circuits (Chapter 42.5).

**Q169. Why must synthetic underwear not be worn under arc-rated clothing?**
It melts in an arc flash and causes severe burns (Chapter 42.5).

**Q170. In what order are portable earths applied and removed?**
Apply the earth clamp to the earth bar first, then the phases with the insulated stick; remove phases first and earth last (Chapter 42.7).

**Q171. Name four back-feed sources.**
DG through changeover, UPS and inverters, capacitors, VFD DC bus, orange interlock wires from other panels, PT secondaries, motors driven by their load (Chapter 42.8).

**Q172. Which lead of a meter is connected first?**
The black (common) lead first and removed last (Chapter 42.9).

## E.14 Programming, stores and earthing

**Q173. What does program name J2617-B05 mean?**
Job J2617, press brake (B) program number 05 (Chapter 43.2).

**Q174. Where is a laser lead-in placed?**
Outside the part contour (inside holes, in the scrap area), so that pierce marks are off the part (Chapter 43.3).

**Q175. What may an operator change in a CNC program?**
Only set-up corrections such as angle corrections and offsets, informing the programming office; geometry, tooling and sequence changes are made by the programming office only (Chapter 43.8).

**Q176. What minimum web between punched holes is allowed?**
At least the sheet thickness, 1.5 x thickness for stainless steel (Chapter 43.4).

**Q177. What is checked when a contactor is received in stores?**
Full catalogue number, rating, coil voltage, poles and auxiliary contacts against the purchase order; condition; quantity (Chapter 44.1).

**Q178. Which components have their serial numbers recorded on the job?**
ACBs, MCCBs with electronic releases, protection relays, drives, PLC CPUs, energy meters and metering CTs (Chapter 44.5).

**Q179. What size protective conductor is needed for a 70 mm2 phase conductor?**
S / 2 = 35 mm2 (same material) (Chapter 45.2.1).

**Q180. How far is the current spike placed in the fall of potential test of a 3 m electrode?**
At least 10 x the electrode length (30 m or more), with the potential spike at 62 % of that distance (Chapter 45.4.1).

**Q181. Can a clamp-on earth tester measure a single isolated electrode?**
No; it needs a parallel earth path in a multi-electrode grid (Chapter 45.4.2).

**Q182. What is the maximum earth fault loop impedance for instantaneous tripping of a C16 MCB at 230 V?**
About 1.4 ohm (230 V / 160 A) (Chapter 45.6).

## E.15 Improvement, tools and new machines

**Q183. What three factors make up OEE?**
Availability, performance and quality: OEE = A x P x Q (Chapter 46.2).

**Q184. What is the main idea of SMED?**
Separate internal from external set-up work, move as much as possible to external (done while the machine runs), then simplify the internal steps (Chapter 46.3).

**Q185. Give two poka-yoke examples from the plant.**
Colour-coded ferrule bins and dies; paint mark after torquing busbar bolts; separate bins for coil voltages; disconnection tags listed for reconnection after HV test (Chapter 46.4).

**Q186. What are the four steps of PDCA?**
Plan, do, check, act (Chapter 46.5).

**Q187. Why must a torque wrench not be used to loosen bolts?**
It damages the calibration (Chapter 50.2).

**Q188. How are portable power tools protected electrically?**
Used on sockets with 30 mA RCCBs, double insulated preferred, inspected before use and tested every 6 months (Chapter 50.3.1).

**Q189. How is drilling done near mounted devices?**
Only with supervisor approval; cover devices; collect swarf with a magnet or vacuum; deburr and paint the edge; prefer hydraulic knock-out punches (Chapter 50.4).

**Q190. When is a newly installed precision machine re-levelled?**
One month after installation (foundations settle), then yearly (Chapter 49.3).

**Q191. What must be updated when a new machine is registered?**
Asset code and history card, machine chapter and daily check sheet, master PM schedule, lubrication chart, spare parts list, compliance register if applicable, alarm codes, skill matrix and backups (Chapter 49.6).

**Q192. What is the phase voltage of the 415 V plant supply?**
About 240 V nominal (415 / 1.732); commonly quoted as 230 V (Chapter 48.4).

**Q193. What decides the direction of rotation of a three-phase motor?**
The phase sequence; swapping any two phases reverses it (Chapter 48.4).

**Q194. What is the difference between neutral and earth?**
Neutral carries current in normal operation as the return conductor; earth carries current only during a fault to trip the protection. Never use one for the other (Chapter 48.5).

**Q195. Which nameplate value is used to set a motor overload relay?**
The full load current (A) at the actual supply voltage and connection (Chapter 48.9, 19.6.1).
