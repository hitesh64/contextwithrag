# 39. Statutory Inspections and Compliance Register

Some equipment and activities of the plant must be inspected or licensed by law or by authorised competent persons. The maintenance manager keeps the compliance register below and reviews it monthly. The exact legal requirements depend on the state and the applicable rules; the safety officer confirms them with the authorities, and the intervals below are the plant's planned intervals.

## 39.1 Compliance register

| Item | Requirement | Interval | Responsible | Record |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lifting machines (CRANE-16, jib cranes) and lifting tackle | Examination and load test by competent person | Crane: yearly; tackle: every 6 months | Maintenance manager | Certificates; colour tags on tackle |
| Pressure vessels (air receiver of COMP-15) | Examination and hydraulic test by competent person | As per rules (examination yearly in this plant) | Maintenance manager | Certificate |
| Safety valves | Test and setting | Yearly | Maintenance | Test record, seal |
| Electrical installation | Inspection by electrical inspector / as per electricity rules; licensed supervisor in charge | As per rules | Electrical in-charge | Inspection report |
| Earth pits | Resistance test | Every 6 months (before and after monsoon) | Electrical in-charge | Earth pit register |
| Fire extinguishers | Inspection and refilling as per standard | Monthly inspection; refill and pressure test as per type | Safety officer | Tags, register |
| Fire hydrant / sprinkler (if installed) | Test | Quarterly | Safety officer | Register |
| Fire NOC | Renewal | As per authority | Plant head | NOC |
| Consent to operate (pollution control) | Air and water consent; effluent standards | As per consent validity | Plant head, environment in-charge | Consent, monthly effluent reports |
| Hazardous waste authorisation and manifests | Disposal through authorised agency | Each disposal; returns yearly | Environment in-charge | Manifests |
| DG set | Emission and noise norms; stack | As per rules | Maintenance | Reports |
| Workplace monitoring (noise, dust, fumes) | Measurement | Yearly | Safety officer | Reports |
| Medical examination of workers in hazardous processes (paint shop, welding) | Pre-employment and periodic | Yearly | HR, safety officer | Medical records |
| Factory licence | Renewal | As per rules | Plant head | Licence |
| Gas pipeline and burners (PNG) | Inspection by gas supplier / competent person | Yearly | Maintenance | Report |
| Laser safety (LASER-03) | Interlock tests, labels, trained persons | Daily interlock test; yearly review | Maintenance | Check sheets |

## 39.2 Calendar

The compliance calendar is posted in the maintenance office with every due date of the year. Items due within 30 days are highlighted in the monthly maintenance meeting. An expired certificate on lifting equipment or a pressure vessel means the equipment must be taken out of use until it is examined.

## 39.3 Contractors

Contractors working in the plant (burner service, crane inspection, electrical contractors, effluent plant operators) must:

- Receive the safety induction.
- Work under a permit to work.
- Use their own calibrated instruments and certified equipment, with copies of certificates given to the maintenance office.
- Follow the plant's LOTO procedure with their own personal locks.

!! HINGLISH: Crane, slings, air receiver, safety valve, earth pit, fire extinguisher, pollution consent jaisi cheezon ka kaanooni inspection samay par hona zaroori hai. Certificate expire ho jaye to wo equipment use mat karo. Contractor ko bhi induction, permit aur apna lock chahiye.
