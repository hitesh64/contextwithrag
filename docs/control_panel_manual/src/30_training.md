# 30. Training and Skill Development

## 30.1 Induction for new employees

Every new employee, including contract workers and trainees, completes the induction before entering the shop floor.

| Day | Topic |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Plant tour, rules (Chapter 3.1), PPE issue and use, emergency procedure, first aid, near miss reporting |
| Day 1 | LOTO awareness: what a lock means; never remove someone else's lock |
| Day 2 | 5S, floor markings, material handling, crane zone rules |
| Day 2 | Quality basics: job card, drawing revision, first-off, red tag and quarantine |
| Day 3 onwards | Job training in the assigned bay under a level 4 trainer |

A short written or oral test is done at the end of day 2. The induction record is signed by the trainee, the trainer and the safety officer.

## 30.2 Machine operator training

Machine training follows four steps for each machine:

1. **Explain**: the trainer explains the purpose, parts, hazards and controls of the machine (using the machine chapter of this manual).
2. **Show**: the trainer demonstrates start-up, a job, checks and shutdown.
3. **Practise**: the trainee operates under direct supervision for at least the number of shifts in the table.
4. **Assess**: the supervisor observes the trainee doing a complete job alone and asks questions on safety, quality checks and alarms. If passed, the trainee's name is added to the machine's authorisation list.

| Machine | Minimum supervised shifts | Assessment includes |
|---|---|---|
| SHEAR-01 | 5 | Blade gap selection, finger guard, LOTO for stuck strip |
| PUNCH-02 | 10 | Tool loading, clearance selection, force calculation, clamp dead zone |
| LASER-03 | 15 | Window and nozzle care, height calibration, parameter selection, fire risk |
| BRAKE-04 | 20 | Laser guard test, V-die selection, force check, bend deduction, angle correction |
| WELD-05 / WELD-06 | 5 each | Peel test, tip dressing; MIG parameters, defects |
| PEM-07 | 3 | Fastener table, safety test, push-out test |
| PTL-08 | 10 | Titrations, chemical safety, bath additions |
| PCL-09 | 10 | Earthing, gun settings, DFT, oven and fire safety |
| BUS-10 | 10 | Bending rules, flat length, two-hand control |
| WIRE-11 / CRIMP-12 / MARK-13 | 5 | Strip check, crimp checks and pull test, ferrule codes |
| TEST-14 | Testing engineers only, 30 shifts with a senior engineer | All tests of Chapter 20, HV safety |
| CRANE-16 | 5 plus slinging course | Pre-use check, signals, slinging |

## 30.3 Technician development path

| Level | Skills |
|---|---|
| Technician trainee | Safety, LOTO, basic tools, measuring instruments, reading simple drawings, assisting PM |
| Technician | PM tasks alone, simple breakdowns (sensors, contactors, filters, hoses), recording |
| Senior technician | Fault finding on CNC machines, hydraulics and pneumatics, PLC diagnostics (read only), drives parameters, root cause analysis |
| Maintenance engineer / supervisor | Planning, PM programme improvement, spare parts, contractor management, training others |

Recommended external training: electrical supervisor licence, hydraulics course, PLC basics course, thermography level 1, vibration analysis category 1, crane and slinging safety.

## 30.4 Safety refresher

- LOTO refresher: yearly for all authorised persons, with a practical demonstration.
- Fire drill: twice a year for the whole plant.
- First aid refresher: every 2 years for nominated first aiders.
- Electrical safety: yearly for wiremen, testing engineers and electrical technicians.

## 30.6 Toolbox talks

A toolbox talk is a 5-minute safety talk at the start of the shift on one topic. Topics are chosen from recent near misses, incidents and case studies (Chapter 28). Examples:

- "Why we never reach past the press brake light curtain."
- "How to discharge a VFD before touching terminals."
- "Earth studs: why the masking cap matters."
- "Sheet edges: cut-resistant gloves, always."
- "Oil injection injuries: never feel for a hydraulic leak."

Attendance is recorded on the toolbox talk sheet.

!! HINGLISH: Naye karmchari ko 2 din induction ke baad hi shop floor par kaam milta hai. Har machine ki training chaar kadam mein: samjhao, dikhao, practice karwao, assess karo. Assessment pass hone par hi machine ki authorisation list mein naam aata hai. Har shift ki shuruaat mein 5 minute ka toolbox talk hota hai.
