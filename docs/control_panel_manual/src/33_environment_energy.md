# 33. Environment, Health and Energy

## 33.1 Environmental aspects of the plant

| Activity | Environmental aspect | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-treatment (PTL-08) | Chemical effluent, sludge | Effluent treatment plant, authorised disposal of sludge, daily effluent pH record |
| Powder coating (PCL-09) | Powder waste, oven flue gas | Recovery system, closed waste bins, burner tuning yearly |
| Laser cutting and welding | Metal fumes | Fume extractors with filters; filter cartridges disposed as hazardous waste |
| Machines with hydraulics | Waste oil, oily rags | Waste oil drums, sold to authorised re-refiner; oily rags in closed red bins |
| Compressor | Condensate with oil | Oil-water separator before drain |
| Packing | Wood, plastic, cardboard | Reuse, segregation for recycling |
| Electrical scrap | Old devices, cables, batteries, lamps | E-waste to authorised recycler; batteries returned |
| Diesel generator | Exhaust, noise, diesel spills | Acoustic enclosure, stack height, drip tray |

### 33.1.1 Waste segregation

| Bin / container | Colour | Waste |
|---|---|---|
| General recyclable | Blue | Paper, cardboard, clean plastic |
| Metal scrap | Grey | MS scrap (see Chapter 5.6 for other metals) |
| Hazardous solid | Red, closed | Oily rags, used filters, paint waste, chemical containers |
| Food and organic | Green | Canteen waste only |
| E-waste | Yellow, closed | Electronic scrap, lamps, batteries |

## 33.2 Occupational health

| Hazard | Effect | Control | Health check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Noise above 85 dB(A) | Hearing loss | Ear protection, enclosures, rotation | Audiometry yearly for exposed workers |
| Welding fumes and laser fumes | Lung disease, metal fume fever | Extraction, masks | Lung function test yearly for welders |
| Powder dust | Respiratory irritation | Booth airflow, masks | Lung function test yearly |
| Chemicals | Skin and eye injury, dermatitis | PPE, washing facilities | Skin check; eye check |
| Manual handling | Back injuries | Lifting aids, two-person lifts, training | - |
| Vibration (grinders) | Hand-arm vibration | Low-vibration tools, rotation, breaks | - |
| Heat (ovens, summer) | Heat stress | Drinking water, breaks, ventilation | - |
| Eye strain (inspection, fine wiring) | Fatigue, errors | Good lighting (500 lux at wiring benches, 750 lux at inspection), magnifiers | Eye test yearly |

### 33.2.1 Ergonomics at wiring and assembly benches

- Benches at a height that allows work at elbow level; adjustable stands for mounting plates.
- Anti-fatigue mats where workers stand for long periods.
- Panels rotated or raised on stands so that wiring near the floor or the top is done without kneeling or overhead reaching for long periods.
- Short breaks and job rotation for repetitive work (crimping, ferrule fitting).

## 33.3 Energy use and saving

| Energy user | Share of plant electricity (typical) | Saving actions |
|---|---|---|
| Ovens (PTL-08 dry-off, PCL-09 cure) | Gas, plus fans | Load the conveyor fully, avoid empty running, keep doors and air curtains working, insulation repairs |
| Compressed air (COMP-15) | 20 to 30 % | Leak repair (Chapter 23.2.6), lower pressure where possible (each 1 bar less saves about 7 % energy), switch off at night |
| Laser cutting (LASER-03) | 15 to 20 % | Good nesting, switch laser to standby during long breaks |
| Hydraulic machines | 10 to 15 % | Switch off pumps when idle more than 15 minutes (or use auto-standby), keep oil at proper temperature |
| Lighting | 5 to 10 % | LED lighting, switch off unused bays, daylight |
| Spray pumps of PTL-08 | 5 to 10 % | Run only during production; VFD on pumps |

### 33.3.1 Power factor and maximum demand

The utility charges for maximum demand (kVA) and penalises low power factor. The APFC panel keeps the PF above 0.98. The utility operator watches the maximum demand meter: avoid starting the ovens, the compressor and the laser at the same moment at the start of the shift; stagger them by 10 minutes.

## 33.4 Housekeeping and fire load

- Keep packing material and powder boxes in their designated stores, not in the bays.
- Remove oily rags and paper from the shop floor every shift.
- Keep at least 1 metre clear in front of electrical panels and fire equipment.
- Keep the paint shop free of combustible waste; clean powder deposits daily.
